# FAQ

Short answers to common questions.

## Is poof.fun live?

No. Concept stage: this website is a UI and simulation. No contracts are deployed and no real funds move here.

## Which chain is poof.fun for?

Solana. The wallet button runs in devnet display-only mode in this simulation; a real deployment would target Solana mainnet after the [Roadmap](/docs/roadmap) phases complete.

## Why three tokens instead of one?

So that attention to any one token supports all three, and so that holders, creators, and supply-burns each get their own fee stream instead of fighting over one. See [The problem with single-token launches](/docs/problem-single-token).

## Do I receive all three tokens when I buy?

No. You pick one token and receive only that one. The other two legs go to protocol inventory. See [How a buy works](/docs/how-a-buy-works).

## Will the three tokens stay equally priced?

Not guaranteed. Equal flows into separate pools can still diverge, and the site warns when the market-cap gap passes 5%. See [Equal valuation](/docs/equal-valuation).

## Do burns push the price up?

Not necessarily. Burns reduce supply, but price is set by trading. See [Three fee identities](/docs/three-fee-identities).

## Can I launch a real narrative here?

No. The launch form creates a local draft in your browser for experimentation. Nothing is deployed.

## What does the 0.25% protocol fee fund?

Protocol development, security audits, and operations. It is the protocol's only revenue — leg fees belong to holders, creators, and burns. See [Fees and revenue model](/docs/fees-and-revenue).

## Where do I start?

Traders: [How a buy works](/docs/how-a-buy-works). Creators: [Creator launch guide](/docs/creator-launch-guide). Skeptics: [Risks and security](/docs/risks-and-security).
