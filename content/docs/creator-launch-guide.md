# Creator launch guide

Launching a narrative means defining one story and three tokens. This is the guide for creators.

## Before you launch

- **One narrative, three characters.** The tokens should feel like a set — DOGREV / WOLFREV / BEARREV work because they rhyme conceptually. Three unrelated tickers will not hold a narrative together.
- **Pick fee destinations deliberately.** The suggested mapping is A → holder rewards, B → creator rewards, C → buyback and burn, but the launch form lets you assign any destination to any token. Remember: you only get **one** creator-rewards stream per narrative, so choose which token carries it.
- **Set starting market caps.** Each token starts with 1,000,000,000 supply priced against its starting cap. Caps do not have to match — but the market-cap gap warning fires past a 5% spread, so near-equal starts behave best.

## The launch form

Under **Launch → Create narrative** you provide:

1. **Narrative name and description** — the story traders buy into.
2. **Per token:** name, ticker, fee destination, starting market cap.
3. **Buy allocation** — three percentages that must total exactly 100. The default is 33.33 / 33.33 / 33.34. Skewing the allocation (e.g. 50 / 30 / 20) concentrates buying pressure on one pool.

## After launch (concept)

In the full concept, the creator-rewards token streams its 1% leg fees to the creator address, viewable under **Portfolio → Rewards**. In this simulation, launches are local drafts stored in your browser — nothing is deployed.

## Creator responsibilities

- Disclose that poof.fun is concept-stage and unaudited.
- Do not promise equal valuations, price floors, or burn-driven appreciation. See [Risks and security](/docs/risks-and-security) for the language to avoid.
- One creator stream per narrative keeps incentives legible. If you need more, launch a second narrative.
