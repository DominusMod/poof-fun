# Risks and security

poof.fun is concept-stage software. This page states the risks plainly — both of the concept and of this website.

## Concept risks

- **No contracts are deployed.** There is nothing to audit yet, and nothing to trust. Any "poof.fun" token or program you encounter today is not this project.
- **Equal valuation is unproven.** The gap mechanism is an open question; assume the three tokens can and will diverge. See [Equal valuation](/docs/equal-valuation).
- **Inventory risk.** Protocol inventory concentrates holdings that could become worthless (narrative dies) or a target (narrative moons). Ownership and wind-down are undecided — see [Open questions](/docs/open-questions).
- **Burns do not guarantee price appreciation.** Supply reduction is not a price floor. Any creator promising otherwise is misrepresenting the mechanism.
- **A shared pool does not automatically give equal valuations.** Proportional flows into separate pools still diverge. Anyone claiming otherwise has not done the math.
- **Memecoins are volatile and often go to zero.** Coordinated buying spreads flow; it does not spread risk away. You can lose everything you put in.

## This website's risks (such as they are)

- This site handles **no real funds** and asks for **no wallet connection** beyond a display-only devnet button. It cannot lose you money because there is nothing to take.
- Mock data and localStorage drafts live in your browser. Clearing site data removes your drafts and claim states.
- The simulation is simplified: no slippage from other traders, no MEV, no fees changing mid-trade, no chain reorgs. Real deployment would face all of these.

## Language to avoid (for creators)

Do not promise: equal prices, price floors, guaranteed burns-driven gains, "risk-free" anything, audit-equivalent safety, or deployment timelines. The roadmap has phases, not dates, deliberately.
