# Trader guide

How to trade narratives as someone buying tokens — not launching them.

## Picking a token

On a narrative page, the three tokens sit side by side with price, market cap, fee destination, fee bucket, and protocol inventory. You pick **one** to receive. Consider:

- **Fee destination.** Holding DOGREV (holder rewards) accrues claimable rewards from every subsequent buy. Holding WOLFREV or BEARREV does not — their fees go to the creator and to burns.
- **Price and impact.** Your whole amount moves your token's pool leg, so check the preview's price impact before signing.
- **The gap.** If the market-cap gap warning is showing, the three tokens have drifted apart. That tells you something about where recent attention went.

## Reading the preview

Every buy shows a **before-you-sign preview**: protocol fee, the three-way split, the tokens you receive, average fill price, per-leg fees, and price impact. The two non-selected legs are labelled **inventory** — they are not yours.

## Tracking your position

**Portfolio → Holdings** values your balances at current engine prices with P&L. **Portfolio → Rewards** shows claimable holder rewards, creator accruals, and the buyback-and-burn history for context on the burn token's supply pressure.

## Simulation honesty

Balances, prices, and rewards on this site are mock data and local math. Nothing here is a real position, and practising on the engine demo is free — use it before risking anything on any real launchpad, poof.fun or otherwise.
